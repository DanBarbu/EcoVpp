Recommended Reading

Books that inspire our thinking on land, community, and living together peacefully.

Land & Community

The Biggest Estate on Earth

Bill Gammage

A landmark study revealing how Aboriginal Australians managed and shaped an entire continent for over 65,000 years through sophisticated fire and land practices — a model of sustainable living in harmony with nature.

“When people and land work together, landscapes flourish for all.”

Ecology & Belonging

Nourishing Terrains

Deborah Bird Rose

A profound exploration of how Indigenous Australians understand country — not merely as land, but as a living, nourishing relationship between people, animals, and place. A meditation on care, reciprocity, and ecological belonging.

“Country is not a backdrop to life — it is life itself, in relationship.”

Reconciliation & Healing

Fire Country

Victor Steffensen

A powerful call to revive Indigenous fire management knowledge — showing how traditional burning practices heal landscapes, rebuild communities, and offer a path to genuine reconciliation between ancient wisdom and the modern world.

“Healing the land and healing relationships between peoples go hand in hand.”
