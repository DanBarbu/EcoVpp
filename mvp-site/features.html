Everything the community needs, in one place.

EcoVPP handles solar sharing, forecasting, smart-appliance nudges, EV charging and monthly billing — so residents just enjoy the savings.

For residents

📱

Simple app

See how much solar you got today, this month, and how much you saved. That's it.

💶

Bill credits

Your share of shared solar appears as a discount on your regular electricity bill — no separate invoice to manage.

🔔

Sunny-hour alerts

Optional notifications when there's plenty of free solar — perfect time to run the washing machine.

🔒

Data stays local

Your consumption data is your business. Nothing leaves the building's system without your consent.

↩️

Opt out anytime

Not for you? Cancel with a click. The others continue unaffected.

🌍

Your carbon report

See how much CO₂ your building saved this year — as a community, and as a household.

For the building manager

📊

Live dashboard

Solar produced, shared, and sold to the grid — updated live. Historical charts for planning.

📄

Compliance reports

Monthly and annual reports formatted for the national regulator. Prepared automatically.

🧾

Fair billing engine

Calculates each resident's share transparently. If a resident disputes a number, you can trace it end-to-end.

🚨

Fault alerts

Get notified early if a panel underperforms, a meter goes offline, or something needs attention.

Add-ons

🔋

Community battery

Store daytime solar for evening consumption. EcoVPP manages when to charge and discharge for best value.

🚗

EV charging

Chargers in the garage prefer solar hours automatically. Multi-vehicle fairness built in.

♨️

Heat pumps

Ramp hot-water production up when the roof is producing — free warmth for the building.

📥

Grid services

Optionally offer the building's flexibility to the local grid operator for extra revenue.

All features work together

Start with just solar sharing. Add a battery or EV charging later. EcoVPP grows with your building.
