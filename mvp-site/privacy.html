2. What we collect and why

2.1 Website visits

Our web server logs limited technical data (truncated IP address, user-agent, requested URL, timestamp) for the strict purpose of operating and securing the website (GDPR art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interest). Logs are retained for a maximum of 30 days and then deleted.

2.2 Contact form

When you submit the contact form we process your name, email, organisation (if provided) and message to reply to your enquiry (art. 6(1)(b) — pre-contractual measures — and 6(1)(a) — consent, where applicable). Contact data is kept for the period reasonably necessary to complete the enquiry and up to 24 months, unless a longer retention is required by law.

2.3 Cookies and local storage

The website uses only strictly-necessary storage:

  • ecovpp.cookies — records that you saw the cookie banner (localStorage).
  • A short-lived language preference (localStorage) so the language switcher remembers your choice.

We do not deploy analytics cookies, advertising cookies or third-party trackers. No consent is legally required for the two items above under art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive because they are strictly necessary for a service explicitly requested by the user.

3. Recipients

We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers. Data is accessed only by authorised staff of the controller and by service providers acting as processors (hosting, email delivery) under a written data-processing agreement (art. 28 GDPR). A list of current processors is available on request.

4. International transfers

Data is processed within the European Economic Area (EEA) by default. Where a processor requires a transfer outside the EEA, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Decision (EU) 2021/914) and, where relevant, on supplementary measures such as encryption at rest and in transit.

5. Retention

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, plus any statutory retention periods (e.g. accounting retention obligations under national tax law).

6. Your rights

You have the right, under GDPR articles 15–22, to:

  • Access your personal data and receive a copy;
  • Rectify inaccurate data;
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten") where applicable;
  • Restrict or object to processing;
  • Data portability;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — the competent authority in your habitual country of residence (e.g. ANSPDCP in Romania, BfDI in Germany, Garante Privacy in Italy, AEPD in Spain).

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email in section 1. We reply within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, art. 12(3) GDPR).

7. Security

We implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk (art. 32 GDPR), including TLS encryption in transit, access controls, patch management and staff confidentiality obligations. In the unlikely event of a personal-data breach, we notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours where required (art. 33 GDPR).

8. Automated decision-making

We do not carry out automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects on you (art. 22 GDPR).

9. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our processing or in the law. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
