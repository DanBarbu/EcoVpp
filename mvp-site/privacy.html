Privacy, in plain language.

The short version: your energy data belongs to you. We only touch what we need to calculate your fair share of the community solar, and we never sell anything.

What we collect

  • Contact details — name, email, apartment, so we can send you your monthly summary.
  • Meter totals — how much electricity your apartment used in each period, so we can calculate your solar share.
  • Basic app usage — anonymous counts of which pages are opened, to improve the experience.

What we don't do

  • Sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers.
  • Track you across other sites.
  • Store your data outside the European Union without explicit consent.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or leave the community entirely. All handled through the app or by emailing us; we reply within five working days.

Cookies

One first-party session cookie when you're signed in. No third-party trackers, no analytics by default.

Contact

Reach us anytime via the contact page.
