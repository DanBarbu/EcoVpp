What every rollout includes

Resident-first design

Each household opts in individually and can leave anytime. No lock-in.

Local hosting

Data stays in the EU. Building-level systems can even run without a permanent internet connection.

Compliance built-in

Reporting to the local utility and regulator is automatic — one less thing for the building manager to worry about.

Fair-share guarantees

The sharing algorithm is transparent. Every resident can see exactly how their share was calculated.
