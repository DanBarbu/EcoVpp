Why it matters

How it works

Four simple steps. No electricians, no new meters on your wall.

1

Solar goes on the roof

One shared system for the whole building. Your building manager or a partner installer handles it.

2

We measure who uses what

Your existing electricity meter reading is enough. No new hardware in your apartment.

3

Solar is shared fairly

Whenever the roof is producing, we split the clean kWh between the apartments using electricity right then.

4

Everyone saves

Each apartment sees the shared solar on its bill as a credit. Extra energy is sold back to the grid, and that money goes to the building too.

What EcoVPP does

A single platform that handles everything the community needs.

☀️

Solar sharing

Distributes rooftop solar across the building fairly, based on when each apartment is actually using electricity.

📊

Fair bill credits

Every resident sees their share of the free solar as a credit on their electricity bill. Transparent, itemised.

🌦️

Smart forecast

Predicts when solar will be abundant so the building can shift laundry, EV charging and hot water to sunny hours automatically.

🔋

Optional battery

Adds a shared battery so evening consumption is still covered by daytime solar.

🚗

EV charging

Charges your car with surplus solar when possible, and pauses to avoid grid overload during peak hours.

🛡️

Privacy-first

Your consumption data stays in your building's system. You choose what leaves it.

Would this work for my building?

Any residential building with a rooftop, at least six units, and willing residents. EU-based buildings are covered by the Renewable Energy Directive that makes energy sharing legal.
