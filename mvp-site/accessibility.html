Accessibility statement

This statement is made pursuant to Directive (EU) 2016/2102 on the accessibility of the websites and mobile applications of public-sector bodies, national transpositions, and the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882). We commit to conform to WCAG 2.1 level AA (EN 301 549 V3).

Conformance status

This website is partially compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA. We are continuously improving accessibility.

Measures taken

  • Semantic HTML with landmarks and headings in reading order.
  • Colour contrast tuned for AA on dark backgrounds.
  • Focus states preserved on all interactive elements.
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, forms and language switcher.
  • Text scales to 200% without loss of content.
  • No auto-playing audio or motion.
  • All form fields have visible labels; error messages are announced in the same field.

Known limitations

Charts on the demo page are currently rendered as decorative visuals. Screen readers receive numeric summaries. If a specific chart is important to you and not accessible, please contact us — we will provide the data on request.

Compatible technology

The site is designed to work with current versions of Firefox, Chrome, Safari and Edge, and with screen readers NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver and TalkBack.

Feedback and contact

Encountered an accessibility barrier? Please write to [accessibility@ecovpp.eu] or use the contact form. We aim to respond within 15 working days.

Enforcement procedure

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the accessibility supervisory authority in your country (e.g. ANDPDCP (Romania), Federal Anti-Discrimination Agency (Germany), AgID (Italy), OADIS (Spain)).
