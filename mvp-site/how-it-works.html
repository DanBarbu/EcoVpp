How it works.

From "let's put solar on the roof" to lower monthly bills for every apartment. Here's the whole path, without the jargon.

The four steps

1

Install solar on the roof

A shared photovoltaic system is installed on the building's rooftop. Typical size for a mid-rise: enough to power daytime lighting, appliances and warm water for every unit.

2

Read the meters

Whether your meter is a modern smart meter or an old dial one, EcoVPP can read it. You get a simple app that shows what your apartment consumed.

3

Share the sunshine

Whenever the roof is producing, the platform assigns each kWh of solar to whoever is using electricity in that moment — proportionally to demand. Nothing wasted.

4

See it on the bill

Every month, each apartment sees a "solar received" line item that reduces its bill. If the roof produced more than the building used, the surplus is sold to the grid and the money is credited back.

What you need in your apartment

✅

An electricity meter

The one you already have. No installation work in your unit.

✅

A phone or a laptop

Optional. You'll get monthly emails whether you use the app or not.

✅

Agreement to share

Community energy sharing is opt-in. Each resident signs up individually.
